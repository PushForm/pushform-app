# Signing in and account details

## Sign in with an email code or link

Enter your email address on the sign-in screen. Request the sign-in email, then enter its code or use its sign-in link if one is provided. Requesting the email alone does not sign you in.

Codes can contain 6 to 10 digits; use the full code from your email. If it expires, request another. Resend becomes available after a short wait. You can change the email address if it was entered incorrectly.

## Complete your account profile

After signing in, complete the profile step when shown. Enter your first and last name, choose individual or company, and choose your industry. A company profile also needs the company name and size. Accept the Terms of Use to continue.

A profile error leaves this step available for correction. If updated terms need acceptance, the step can appear again with your saved details prefilled.

## Edit account details

Open your account control in the header. Edit and save the profile in the account dialog. Your profile name can appear in document auto-text; changes update those live fields.

If you use an individual account, company-only fields are not required. Closing account details does not close your documents.

## Work offline or sign in again

Local editing can continue offline while your sign-in session remains valid. If the session expires, the workspace locks until you sign in. Open documents and undo history remain in memory for the same account in the same page.

Signing out signs out other tabs sharing that browser session, but not other devices. Signing in as a different account uses that account's own workspace and recovery data.
