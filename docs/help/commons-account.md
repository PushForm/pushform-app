# Your Commons account and downloads

You can browse Commons without signing in. To download a model, choose **Download ZIP** and sign in with your PushForm email account. New users complete the same **Tell us about yourself** form used by the editor: name, account details, industry and acceptance of the site Terms of Use. If you already completed that account setup, it is not repeated for every download.

The initial form also offers **Send me PushForm news and updates by email (optional)**. This checkbox starts unchecked. Leaving it unchecked does not prevent downloading or using PushForm. You can change your email-updates choice from your Commons account later.

A public creator profile is optional and separate from your private account details. Downloading does not require publishing your name, bio, avatar or projects to the community.

Downloads contain the original file and its attribution notices in a ZIP. Keep the license and creator credits when reusing the model. If sign-in or account setup fails, retry from the displayed message; a failed download leaves existing projects unchanged.
