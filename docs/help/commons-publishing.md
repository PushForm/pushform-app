# Publish your model in Commons

## Share a saved editor version

In the editor, choose **Save version** in the header or Main menu, or press Ctrl+S (Command+S on macOS). Add optional private change notes. **Share with the community** starts unchecked. Check it and choose **Save & continue to publishing**.

The private cloud revision is saved first. The publishing dialog uses that fixed snapshot, including when later editor changes occur. Choose the whole model or the captured selection when available, enter public listing details, and review the license and credits. Create a public profile on your first publication. Public handles use 3–32 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens, start with a letter and end with a letter or number. Enter the handle without an @. If a handle is already taken, choose another. Cover images are optional. Choose **Review publication**, confirm your rights, and then **Publish**. Cancelling retains the private revision and any publishing draft. Cloud revision numbers and Commons revision numbers are separate.

Publishing later changes requires another explicit save and publication. Saving alone never updates a public model. Required upstream licenses and original creator notices travel with imported content; incompatible or unsupported publication terms must be resolved before publishing.

## Publish through the Commons website

Sign in and complete the initial PushForm account setup. Create a public Commons profile if you want to publish original work under your name. This public profile is separate from the private name and account details used for signup.

Choose **Publish model**, enter the model details and choose **My original work** or **Redistributed with permission**. Original work offers CC BY 4.0, CC BY-NC 4.0 or CC0 1.0. For redistribution, enter the original creator, source and exact upstream license. Add every applicable asset license and attribution notice; the uploader does not automatically become the original creator.

Choose **Save private draft**, then attach the model files. Each file can be up to 128 MiB; a revision can hold 20 files and 512 MiB total. Pending uploads must finish or be removed before publication. Confirm your redistribution rights and choose **Publish model**. Automatic checks determine whether the files and metadata are complete; ordinary publication does not wait for admin approval. Saving or uploading alone keeps the draft private.

Use **Your models** to reopen a draft, create a correction or withdraw your publication. Corrections create new immutable revisions; the previous published revision stays visible until the correction is published. Attach the files for the new revision again and preserve all required license and credit notices. Withdrawing stops new downloads, while existing copies keep the rights granted by their licenses.

## Add an optional preview image

After saving a private draft, use **Preview image** to attach one PNG or JPEG. The image must be no larger than 8 MiB, 8,192 pixels on either side, or 20 megapixels. Remove the current image before replacing it. Include the image’s required licenses and creator credits in the model details. An image alone cannot be published as a model.

Commons generates its own thumbnail from supported geometry when no image is supplied, using the same front-right-top isometric orientation as the editor. An uploaded image becomes the listing image; the interactive geometry preview remains available. The image stays private until you publish and becomes unavailable when the model is withdrawn.

## Preview from the editor

When sharing from PushForm, frame your model before saving and continuing to publication. The preview uses Bright studio lighting with your current zoom, framing, roll, orientation and projection. It renders the exact saved revision without changing your working view or the model's saved lighting. The preview uploads with the private draft and becomes its listing image; you can replace it with a cover photo.

A file uploaded directly to Commons receives a Bright studio preview from a fitted front-right-top Iso view. Both paths preserve supported materials and embedded textures. Commons does not offer a lighting selector.
