# Publish your model in Commons

## Share a saved editor version

In the editor, choose **Save version** in the header or Main menu, or press Ctrl+S (Command+S on macOS). Add optional private change notes. **Share with the community** starts unchecked. Check it and choose **Save & continue to publishing**.

The private cloud revision is saved first. The publishing dialog uses that fixed snapshot, including when later editor changes occur. Choose the whole model or the captured selection when available, enter public listing details, and review the license and credits. Create a public profile on your first publication. Public handles use 3–32 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens, start with a letter and end with a letter or number. Enter the handle without an @. If a handle is already taken, choose another. Cover images are optional. Open the **Collections** dropdown to check any of your collections to add the model to, or type a name and choose **Add** to create a new private collection. Choose **Publish model** directly from the details screen. Publishing confirms that you have the necessary rights under the Terms of Use; there is no separate review step or rights checkbox. Cancelling retains the private revision and any publishing draft. Cloud revision numbers and Commons revision numbers are separate.

## Publish later changes to a shared model

Publishing later changes requires another explicit save and publication. Saving alone never updates a public model. Copies retain their original license versions and creator notices. Republishing a copy does not add the default original-work license, and repeated copies or draft saves do not duplicate identical license notices. Required upstream licenses and original creator notices travel with imported content; incompatible or unsupported publication terms must be resolved before publishing.

## Publish through the Commons website

Sign in and complete the initial PushForm account setup. The first time you publish, create a public Commons profile; your models are published under it. This public profile is separate from the private name and account details used for signup.

Choose **Upload model** and drop or choose one model file. While it uploads and its preview is prepared, enter a title, a category and an optional description, then choose a license: one of the Creative Commons 4.0 licenses, CC0 1.0 (public domain), or **Other** to describe your own terms. CC BY 4.0 is selected to start, and each choice shows a short summary of what others may do. A copy of a Commons model keeps its original licenses and credits instead of offering a license choice; keep the original creator in **Creator credit** when you redistribute someone else's work.

A model file can be up to 128 MiB. Your details are kept in a private draft; **Save draft** saves them without publishing. The upload must finish before you can publish. Choose **Publish model**; this confirms under the Terms of Use that you have the right to share the model under the license you chose. Automatic checks determine whether the files and metadata are complete; ordinary publication does not wait for admin approval. Saving or uploading alone keeps the draft private.

## Manage your published models

Use **My models** to edit or publish a draft, view a published model, withdraw it or delete a draft. Editing a published model keeps its files; **Publish changes** creates a new revision, and the previous published revision stays visible until then. Keep all required license and credit notices. Withdrawing stops new downloads, while existing copies keep the rights granted by their licenses.

## Add an optional preview image

In the upload dialog, use **Add a cover photo** to attach one PNG or JPEG. The image must be no larger than 8 MiB, 8,192 pixels on either side, or 20 megapixels. Use **Replace cover photo** to change it. Include the image’s required licenses and creator credits in the model details. An image alone cannot be published as a model.

Commons generates its own thumbnail from supported geometry when no image is supplied, using the same front-right-top isometric orientation as the editor. An uploaded image becomes the listing image; the interactive geometry preview remains available. The image stays private until you publish and becomes unavailable when the model is withdrawn.

## Preview from the editor

When sharing from PushForm, frame your model before saving and continuing to publication. The preview uses Bright studio lighting with your current zoom, framing, roll, orientation and projection. It renders the exact saved revision without changing your working view or the model's saved lighting. The preview uploads with the private draft and becomes its listing image; you can replace it with a cover photo.

A file uploaded directly to Commons receives a Bright studio preview from a fitted front-right-top Iso view. Both paths preserve supported materials and embedded textures. Commons does not offer a lighting selector.
