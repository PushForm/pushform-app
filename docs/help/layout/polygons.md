# Polygons in Layout

## Draw an eight-sided polygon

1. Choose **Polygon** from the **Draw** group in the toolbar. If the button shows Circumscribed Polygon, open its small arrow and choose Polygon.
2. Type **8s** and press **Enter**. A field opens beside the pointer as you type. You can also enter **8** in the **Sides** field in the Polygon tool pane at the top of the right-side panel.
3. Click to place the center, then click again to set the radius and direction. You can also drag from the center to the radius point.

You can set the sides before placing the center or while positioning the radius. The preview updates as the count changes. The count stays in use for later polygons until you change it. Counts from 3 to 100 are supported.

## Find the Polygon button

In Layout, the Polygon button is in the **Draw** toolbar group, after Circle. Its small arrow opens **Polygon** and **Circumscribed Polygon**. The button remembers the last variant you used. If it is offscreen, scroll the toolbar horizontally. You can also find either variant in command search.

## Choose an inscribed or circumscribed polygon

**Polygon** places its vertices on a reference circle. Your second point places a vertex.

**Circumscribed Polygon** places its sides against the inside reference circle. Your second point places the midpoint of a side. This is useful when the radius should measure to a flat side.

Both variants start with six sides and share the side count you enter. Hold **Alt** (**Option** on macOS) to temporarily use the other variant; release it to return.

## Set the radius and orientation

After placing the center, type a radius into the Radius field and press Enter. For Polygon this measures from the center to a vertex. For Circumscribed Polygon it measures from the center to a side.

The reference point also sets the orientation. As you move it, it snaps horizontally or vertically from the center when close enough. The dashed reference circle and radius help you see how the polygon will be placed.
