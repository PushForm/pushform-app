# Delete and erase geometry in Modeling

## Delete or remove a model object

Choose **Select** with **Space**, select the object, then press **Delete** or **Backspace** with focus in the modeling canvas. Delete removes eligible selected sketch geometry, whole solids, unlocked groups and components, images, guides, dimensions and text. To get rid of several items, select them together before pressing Delete. **Ctrl+Z**, or **Cmd+Z** on macOS, undoes the deletion.

A solid's individual boundary faces and edges cannot be deleted this way. Select the whole solid to remove the solid, or use Push/Pull to change a face. Unlock locked groups or components first. Keys typed inside chat, a field or the Object browser do not invoke canvas deletion; click the canvas without closing chat and select the intended object.

## Erase lines with the Eraser tool

Choose **Eraser** in the toolbar, or press **E** with focus on the modeling canvas. Click or drag across sketch edges and release to erase the touched edges in one undoable step. The Eraser also removes eligible dividing edges, guides, dimensions and text. Erasing a dividing line on a solid face merges the regions it separated.

A solid's actual boundary edge cannot simply be erased. The Eraser offers Shift to hide or Ctrl to soften/smooth; use Push/Pull when you want to change a face's shape. Use Select and Delete to remove an entire solid, group or component instead of trying to erase its boundary edges.
