## Ask AI Chat

Open **AI Chat** from the left menu bar in either editor, or with the chat button on Home. Ask what you want to do, for example "How do I draw an eight-sided polygon in Layout?"

When it can, AI Chat highlights the control you need with a short glow. It won't click anything or change your document for you.

## Report a problem or request a feature

Tell AI Chat what you did and what happened, or describe the feature you'd like. Before it files anything, it shows a short summary for you to check and confirm.

When you report a problem, it helps to include your version. Search for **About PushForm** with **Ctrl+K** (or **Cmd+K** on macOS) to see it.
