PushForm runs in your browser or as a desktop app. Both use the same account and the same cloud files.

## In your browser

Go to [app.pushform.io](https://app.pushform.io). There's nothing to install.

## On your desktop

Download the installer for your platform from [pushform.io/download](https://pushform.io/download).

On macOS, open the downloaded `.dmg` file and drag **PushForm** onto **Applications**. When copying finishes, eject the disk image and open PushForm from your Applications folder.

When a desktop update is ready, a notice offers **Restart to update**. PushForm saves your open documents before it restarts. To check yourself, search for **Check for updates** with **Ctrl+K** (or **Cmd+K** on macOS).

## Sign in

Enter your email address and request a sign-in email. Then type the code from that email, or use its sign-in link if it has one. Codes are 6 to 10 digits long. If yours expires, request another.

The first time you sign in, PushForm asks for your name, whether you're an individual or a company, and your industry. Accept the Terms of Use to continue.

## Start a document

PushForm has two editors:

- **Modeling** for 3D work.
- **Layout** for drawings made from your models: plans, elevations, sections and assembly views, with dimensions, text and tables.

Choose the **+** button and pick **Model** or **Layout**. Each document gets its own tab and its own Undo history.
